# Measured by Nova Context

## Active Terms

- **Measured by Nova**: The canonical product name for the local-first,
  measurement-driven spatial reconstruction and digital viewing system.
- **Measurements**: Primary authority for geometry, scale, placement, and
  dimensions.
- **Structured photos**: Secondary evidence for material, texture, condition,
  context, camera alignment, and validation; never implicit geometry authority.
- **Blender geometry**: Renderable truth only after human review and model lock.
- **Export**: Deterministic packaging and presentation of locked Blender
  artifacts; it may not infer, reconstruct, or mutate geometry.
- **Measured Verified**: A public classification available only to a complete,
  unchanged native capture package whose project, object, capture protocol,
  kit, commissioning party, and signature have been verified.
- **Measured Reference**: The mandatory public classification for manual
  uploads and evidence that does not satisfy the native signed-capture contract.
- **Partially Verified**: A deterministic public classification that names the
  exact verified evidence scopes while required scopes remain unverified.
- **Disputed**: A public classification that preserves provenance while showing
  the exact evidence scopes under an active dispute.

## Avoided Terms

- **Codex Blender MCP**: Historical project name; use only for migration or
  compatibility references.
- **CAD system**: Incorrect product classification. Measured by Nova does not
  claim CAD, BIM, surveying, structural approval, or fabrication authority.

## Relationships

- Measured by Nova is governed by Namaka and Axiome Core.
- An LLM may orchestrate the pipeline but is never an authority for geometry,
  evidence, review state, or export validity.
- Internal risk and fidelity scores may prioritize review or automation but may
  never determine or upgrade a public trust classification.
- Customer ratings remain separate from capture verification and are visible
  only after the configured minimum evidence threshold is met.
